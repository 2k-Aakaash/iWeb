# iWeb: a macOS-style New Tab page for Chrome

**iWeb turns every new tab into a calm, beautiful desktop**, with Liquid Glass, live widgets, a Control Center, built-in apps and instant search. It's free, private, and runs entirely in your browser.

[**⬇ Download the latest release**](https://github.com/2k-Aakaash/iWeb/releases/latest) · [**Try it in your browser**](https://iweb-two.vercel.app/) · [Report an issue](https://github.com/2k-Aakaash/iWeb/issues)

---

## Why iWeb?

Most new tab pages are a search box and a grid of shortcuts. iWeb gives you a whole desktop, inspired by the design of macOS 26:

- **Fast:** the desktop is ready in about a third of a second, and heavy features load only when you use them.
- **Private:** your notes, music library, wallpapers and settings stay on your device. No account, no tracking.
- **Yours:** move, resize and arrange every widget, choose your wallpaper and clock style, and back everything up in one file.

## Features

### A real desktop in your browser

- **Liquid Glass design:** frosted, light-bending surfaces across the Dock, windows, widgets and the clock.
- **Menu bar and Dock:** app menus, keyboard shortcuts, and a Dock for your favourite sites and apps.
- **Dynamic Island:** see what's playing, and a running timer, at the top of the screen.
- **Smooth start:** each new tab opens with a quick login-style reveal.

### Live widgets

- **Weather, currency, gold & silver, stocks and news**, with automatic updates.
- **Smart Stack:** several widgets in one space; swipe or scroll to flip between them.
- **Edit Widgets:** drag, resize and snap widgets into place, or add new ones from the widget gallery.

### Control Center

One click from the menu bar: Now Playing, volume and sound output, wallpaper brightness, Focus mode, Night Shift, a calculator, a timer, screenshots, and more. Rearrange it with Edit Controls.

### Built-in apps

- **Notes:** write in Markdown with a live preview.
- **Music:** play your local music library, with playlists, shuffle and repeat.
- **Markets and News:** follow your stocks and read top stories without leaving the tab.
- **Spotlight (Ctrl K):** search your bookmarks, history and the web, convert currencies and check stock prices as you type.

### Make it yours

- Beautiful wallpapers from Unsplash, or your own photos.
- A glass or vibrant-colour clock, custom fonts, and your name in the greeting.
- One-file backup and restore for all your data.
- Automatic updates from inside the app.

## Install

### As a Chrome extension (recommended)

1. Download **iWeb.Extension.zip** from the [latest release](https://github.com/2k-Aakaash/iWeb/releases/latest) and unzip it.
2. Open `chrome://extensions` and turn on **Developer mode** (top right).
3. Click **Load unpacked** and choose the unzipped folder.
4. Open a new tab and enjoy iWeb.

Works in Chrome, Edge, Brave and other Chromium-based browsers.

### In your browser

Visit **[iweb-two.vercel.app](https://iweb-two.vercel.app/)** and set it as your home page, or install it as an app from your browser's address bar.
